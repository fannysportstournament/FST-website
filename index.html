The Experience

Where Karachi's Passion Meets Elite Competition

Fanny Sports Tournament (FST) is a three-day competitive 6v6 football tournament that brings together passionate footballers from across Karachi. Built with the vision of creating an elite yet accessible football experience, FST combines competitive sport, professional event management, and community spirit. Whether you're stepping onto the pitch or cheering from the sidelines, FST is designed to celebrate the city's love for football.
